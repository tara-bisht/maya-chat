# Launch company is a handful of named specialists

Mass users need official agents across the jobs they already bring to AI (code, study, writing, career, advice, fitness, money, taste). That does not mean launching as “thousands of inbuilt agents,” and it does not mean job-title SKUs on the wall.

**First ship is Maya plus twelve named specialists** covering Work, Learning, Advice, Debate, Everyday. The `agents` table may grow later. If a brief names “YC Pitch Critic” or “Full-Stack Architect,” we cast or map a *person* (Alex, Helena, Kenji) — we do not poster the SKU. Living people and estate names stay off first-party playbills.
